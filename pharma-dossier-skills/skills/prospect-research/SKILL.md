# Pharma Prospect Research

This skill builds a detailed intelligence profile of a pharmaceutical manufacturer before outreach, so every conversation is relevant and shows you understand their regulatory and manufacturing context.

## Objective

Identify whether a prospect is a likely dossier buyer, what molecules they'd need, what markets they're targeting, and who makes formulation and regulatory decisions — so your first message lands.

## Research Framework

### Section 1: Company Profile

**What to find:**
- Company name, HQ city/state, year founded
- Annual revenue estimate or turnover band (small: <₹50Cr, mid: ₹50–500Cr, large: >₹500Cr)
- Number of manufacturing sites, USFDA/WHO-GMP/CDSCO approval status
- Dosage forms they currently manufacture (tablets, capsules, injectables, dry syrups, pellets, etc.)
- Core therapeutic areas (CV, anti-infective, GI, CNS, oncology, etc.)

**Sources:**
- Company website → Products / Manufacturing / About pages
- MCA (Ministry of Corporate Affairs) for Indian companies
- CDSCO site search for their drug licenses
- LinkedIn company page for headcount and recent activity
- IndiaMart / TradeIndia for export listings

### Section 2: Regulatory Footprint

**Key questions:**
- Do they export? To which markets? (Africa, SEA, LATAM, EU, US?)
- Do they have WHO-GMP, NAFDAC, PPB, ANVISA, or EU GMP approvals?
- Are they ANDA filers or CTD applicants?
- Have they recently hired a Regulatory Affairs or QA head? (signals expansion)
- Any recent Form-483s, warning letters, or WHO-PQ suspensions?

**Signals that make them a HOT prospect:**
- Exporting to 5+ countries but limited own formulation IP
- Hiring RA/QA/R&D staff (LinkedIn Jobs)
- Recently added a new dosage form line
- Mid-size company (₹50–500Cr) — big enough to pay, small enough to need ready IP

### Section 3: Decision-Maker Mapping

**Roles to identify:**
| Role | Responsibility | Why They Care |
|------|---------------|---------------|
| R&D Head / VP R&D | Formulation development decisions | Saves 18–24 months of development |
| Regulatory Affairs Head | CDSCO/WHO-GMP filing | Audit-ready documents reduce risk |
| Managing Director / CEO | P&L, expansion decisions | Faster market entry = revenue |
| Technical Director | Manufacturing scale-up | Validated process = fewer deviations |
| Business Development / Export Head | Tender, export dossier filing | Ready dossiers for new markets |

**How to find them:**
- LinkedIn: search "[Company Name] R&D" or "[Company] Regulatory"
- Company website → Team / Leadership page
- IndiaMart seller profiles often show owner names
- Call the main company number and ask for R&D department

### Section 4: Molecule Fit Analysis

**Match their portfolio to PharmaDossier catalogue:**
- What APIs/finished products do they already make?
- What adjacent molecules would fit their existing manufacturing lines?
- Do they have granulators/pellet coaters/fluid bed dryers? → match to available dossier types
- Are they in a therapeutic area where PharmaDossier has dossiers?

**High-fit signals:**
- They make tablets but want to add pellets → Pantoprazole GR pellet dossier
- They make combination products → Telmisartan+Metformin bilayer dossier
- They export to Africa/SEA → WHO-GMP ready dossiers of interest
- They make paediatrics → Amoxicillin+Clavulanate dry syrup dossier

### Section 5: Trigger Events

**Warm triggers (contact within 48 hours):**
- Just posted a job for R&D Scientist / Formulation Scientist
- Announced new plant or expansion
- Filed for new drug license (CDSCO database)
- Attending CPhI India / CPHI Worldwide (LinkedIn activity)
- Posted content about regulatory challenges or export market entry

**Cold outreach (slower cycle):**
- No recent activity
- Already has in-house R&D but limited bandwidth
- Mid-size, no known export certifications

## Output Format

Produce a prospect brief with these sections:

```
## Prospect Brief: [Company Name]

**Overview**
- Location, size, dosage forms, therapeutic focus

**Regulatory Status**
- WHO-GMP / CDSCO / export approvals held
- Markets they export to

**Decision-Makers**
- Name, title, LinkedIn URL (if found)
- Best contact channel (LinkedIn DM / phone / email)

**Molecule Fit**
- Recommended PharmaDossier products for this prospect
- Rationale (matches their existing lines / target market)

**Trigger Event** (if any)
- What recent activity justifies outreach now

**Recommended Approach**
- WhatsApp / LinkedIn / Cold Call — which to lead with and why
- Personalisation hook for first message
- Objection to anticipate
```
