# Follow-Up Sequences for Dossier Sales

Pharma B2B deals take time. Decision-makers are busy. A lead that goes quiet is not a dead lead — it's a lead that needs the right nudge at the right time.

## Objective

Re-engage prospects who have gone silent, provide them a reason to respond now, and move the conversation toward an NDA signature — without being aggressive or spammy.

## Follow-Up Philosophy

**Rule 1: Always give them something, not just a "checking in" ping.**
Every follow-up should deliver new value: a relevant case study, a price reminder, a question they haven't been asked, or a soft deadline.

**Rule 2: Change the channel each time.**
If the first message was WhatsApp, the second should be LinkedIn or email. Breaking the pattern increases open rates.

**Rule 3: Maximum 4 follow-ups.**
After 4 touches with no response, move to a 90-day nurture and don't chase.

**Rule 4: Never be apologetic.**
Don't say "Sorry to bother you again." You have something valuable — act like it.

## Standard Follow-Up Sequence

### Touch 1 — 48 hours after first contact (Same channel)
**Goal:** Soft reminder — they may have missed the first message

**WhatsApp:**
```
Hi [Name], just following up on my message about the 
[Molecule] dossier. Happy to answer any questions or 
share the contents list if it would help.
```

**LinkedIn:**
```
Hi [Name], circling back on the [Molecule] dossier I 
mentioned. If useful, I can send a 2-page overview — 
takes 5 minutes to review. Let me know.
```

---

### Touch 2 — 5–7 days (New value + different channel)
**Goal:** Provide something new they didn't have before

**Email subject:** "Dossier contents list for [Molecule] — available now"

**Body:**
```
Hi [Name],

Sending along the full contents list for our [Molecule] 
[dosage form] dossier:

Module 1 — Administrative (NDA template, filing checklist)
Module 2 — MFR (BOM, process parameters, IPC limits)  
Module 3 — Analytical (HPLC STP, dissolution method)
Module 4 — PDR (development rationale, blend uniformity data)
Module 5 — Validation (3-batch PVP with acceptance criteria)
Module 6 — Stability (accelerated + LT data, ICH Zone IVb)
Module 7 — SOPs (granulation, coating, cleaning, dissolution)

This is a [Standard/Premium] tier document — not just an 
MFR, a complete regulatory-ready package.

India pricing: ₹[X]–[Y] | Export: $[X]–[Y]

If the molecule and tier look right, I can send the NDA 
draft for your legal team to review — no commitment needed 
at that stage.

Worth a quick call this week?

Anirag | PharmaDossier | +91 70884 19261
```

---

### Touch 3 — 2 weeks (Trigger or urgency)
**Goal:** Create a reason to act now

**WhatsApp:**
```
Hi [Name], one quick update — we've had a couple of 
enquiries for the [Molecule] dossier this week. We 
license each molecule to a maximum of 3 manufacturers 
per region to protect IP exclusivity.

If [Company] is still considering it, worth reserving 
your slot. Want me to send the NDA for review?
```

**OR if they had a stated timeline:**
```
Hi [Name], I believe you mentioned [filing deadline / 
tender submission] is coming up. The dossier + NDA 
process takes about 3–5 days once you decide. Happy 
to expedite if the timeline is tightening.
```

---

### Touch 4 — 4 weeks (Break-up message)
**Goal:** Get a "no" or reopen the conversation

**Email:**
```
Subject: [Company] — closing the loop on [Molecule]

Hi [Name],

I've reached out a few times about the [Molecule] 
dossier and haven't heard back — which usually means 
one of these:

a) The timing isn't right
b) You found another solution  
c) This isn't a priority right now
d) My messages aren't getting through

If it's (c) or (d), no problem at all — I'll check 
back in a few months.

If there's a specific reason it didn't fit, I'd 
genuinely appreciate 1 minute of feedback so I can 
improve what we offer.

Either way, the dossier will be here when you need it.

Anirag | PharmaDossier
```

## Situation-Specific Follow-Ups

### After "Send me more info" (They asked but went silent)
```
Hi [Name], I sent the contents list / product sheet on 
[date]. Wanted to check — did it land in your inbox okay?

If you've had a chance to review and have questions, 
I'm happy to walk through it on a quick call. 15 minutes 
would cover everything.
```

### After a WhatsApp read receipt (Seen but no reply)
```
Hi [Name], saw you had a chance to look at my earlier 
message. Happy to answer any questions or share pricing 
if you want to take it to your team.
```

### After a website form submission
```
Hi [Name], thanks for your enquiry on PharmaDossier.com 
about [molecule / general enquiry].

Before I prepare pricing, quick question: which 
regulatory market are you filing for — CDSCO India, 
WHO-GMP export, or ANDA?
```

### After attending CPhI / exhibition (warm meet)
```
Hi [Name], great meeting you at [CPhI / exhibition]. 

As mentioned, we have the [Molecule] dossier available. 
I'll send the contents list across — let me know when 
you've had a chance to look and we can pick up where 
we left off.
```

## 90-Day Nurture Message (For cold leads)

Send once per month — brief, no pressure:

**Month 1:**
```
Hi [Name], sharing our updated catalogue in case it's 
useful for any upcoming projects. [Month] additions: 
[new molecules]. Happy to send details on any of these.
```

**Month 2:**
```
Hi [Name], quick update — we've added [new molecule / 
new market coverage] to the catalogue. Worth a look if 
you're planning for next year.
```

**Month 3:**
```
Hi [Name], circling back one last time before I stop 
following up. If formulation licensing becomes relevant 
for [Company] in the future, you have my number. 
Best of luck with your projects.
```
